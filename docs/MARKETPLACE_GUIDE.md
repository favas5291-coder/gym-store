# GymDrobe marketplace admin and supplier guide

The portal is feature-gated. Deploying its source does not onboard a real supplier, accept an agreement, send a message, collect a payment, book a courier or execute a transfer. Use the enabled staging build for training until acceptance is complete.

## Admin

1. Open Admin → Suppliers (`/admin/marketplace`). Search/filter existing suppliers before creating one. A supplier and a brand are distinct: one supplier may supply multiple approved brands/categories.
2. Add supplier: enter actual identity/contact, business/pickup/return addresses, approved brands/categories, applicable tax information, specific preparation/settlement times and who funds shipping, payment fees and returns. Internal notes are admin-only. No commission or default commercial agreement is assumed.
3. Generate the private, one-use 48-hour invitation. Share it through the business's approved secure channel yourself; the dashboard sends no message. Do not put the code in a public URL. Admin never chooses or sees the supplier's password.
4. Supplier activates and explicitly accepts the exact terms. Review identity, rights, stock and accepted terms before Approve/reactivate. Suspension hides products and prevents new catalogue/checkout activity while existing orders, resolution records and payouts remain accessible. Reactivation does not silently republish previously hidden products.
5. Product reviews: inspect submitted photos/content/variants/supplier amounts/returns and category details. Set a separate **tax-inclusive customer price for every variant** and the confirmed included tax rate. Do not enter a guessed tax rate. MRP must cover selling price. Preview server coupon effects. A loss budget needs an explicit audited reason; zero prevents unapproved below-supplier pricing. Approve without publishing keeps the existing live product unchanged; Approve and publish updates the real catalogue. Feedback is visible to the supplier.
6. Follow supplier groups under Orders. Supplier accepts/prepares/adds carrier and tracking; admin records actual delivery confirmation. This does not book or contact a courier. Separate shipments appear in the customer tracking page. Mixed orders retain one customer order and separate supplier groups.
7. Under Payment details, review the masked current version. Accessing the full destination requires fresh identity confirmation and an audited purpose. Verify actual ownership outside this software before recording verification. A supplier change creates a new pending version; old payout snapshots remain intact.
8. Earnings: reconcile latest records. ₹500 supplier/₹550 customer yields ₹500 supplier obligation and ₹50 markup before expenses. Full collection, delivery, the purchased return window and the accepted settlement hold must all be satisfied. A COD advance is not full earnings.
9. Select eligible earnings and reserve a manual payout. The reservation is atomic and idempotent. Any previous paid-return recovery is explicitly itemized against future earnings. Download the CSV, check gross eligible amounts, recovery offsets, cash payout and this payout's exact destination version. Review this payout destination from its record, rather than assuming the current profile destination is unchanged.
10. Perform a transfer outside GymDrobe only through the owner's authorized process. After it actually completes, record its real reference/date, full-amount/destination confirmation and optional private proof image. **Recording is not transfer execution or independent bank confirmation.** Cancel a reservation only if its transfer did not complete; this releases cash and recovery offsets. An uncertain actual transfer needs bank reconciliation before retrying.
11. Item resolution: choose the purchased earning, cancellation before dispatch or actually received/inspected return, affected quantity and verified restock quantity/evidence. Server calculates the exact purchased credit/refund. Cancellation after a COD advance reduces unpaid merchandise first; only collected excess is refundable. Use Orders to record an already completed external refund, or a verified remaining COD collection with evidence. Neither action moves money. Original purchased totals, history and completed payouts are preserved. Do not overlap the whole-order and item-resolution workflows.
12. In Orders and shipments, open **Existing catalogue items (no supplier assignment)** for old catalogue items purchased together with supplier items. Choose the purchased item, affected quantity and actual cancellation or inspected-return evidence. Supplier-owned purchased items use their earning controls instead. These records do not create supplier ownership or reduce another supplier’s agreed amount. Record an already completed external refund separately. Restock only recorded reservations whose product ownership is still unassigned; otherwise reconcile stock manually. Purchased shipping is preserved until the shipping-refund rules are approved.
13. Financial reporting records actual platform/supplier-paid costs separately, with evidence and references. Supplier-paid expenses are not automatic deductions. Collections, obligations, markup and expenses are distinct. Missing expenses are unconfirmed; the report is not net profit or a tax filing.

## Supplier

1. Open `/supplier/activate`, enter the private code, review the exact commercial terms and explicitly accept. Set your own password (12–72 characters, max72 UTF-8 bytes). If your email already has a GymDrobe account, sign in to it first; an invitation cannot take it over or reset its credentials. Admin approval is still required.
2. Sign in at `/supplier/login`. The existing account token restores your session after refresh. Use Account settings to change your own password with identity confirmation; older sessions are invalidated. Google confirmation must use the same account and does not switch the current workspace. Customer shopping/Google login remains available.
3. Overview shows actual approved products, review queue, orders needing action, current low stock, pending eligibility, payout balances and activity.
4. Add product: choose an approved brand/category, describe the item, add authorized photos, supplier SKU and each size/colour/pack variant, supplier amount, optional MRP and physical stock. Enter shipping measurements/preparation and confirmed return conditions. Fill relevant clothing size/care or supplement batch/expiry/manufacturer fields. Submit for admin review. Customer prices belong to admin.
5. Drafts and proposed content/price changes do not overwrite approved live content. Read admin feedback and resubmit a corrected draft. Stock uses the separate “Update live stock” control; it preserves approved content/prices. If sales changed quantities after the page loaded, reload before applying a new physical count.
6. Orders and shipments show only your purchased items and necessary fulfilment details. Accept, prepare and enter genuine carrier/tracking details. Admin confirms delivery. You cannot mark a customer collection, approve your own listing, set a selling price or mark yourself paid.
7. Earnings stay pending until collection/delivery/return and settlement conditions qualify. Refunds and reversals may reduce the balance. Completed payout records remain unchanged; later recoveries and future offsets are visible.
8. Submit bank/UPI details using Payment details and confirm your identity. Values are encrypted and masked in normal views. Every change requires admin reverification. Payouts show itemized statements and externally recorded references; they are not automatic bank-transfer confirmations.
9. Business profile changes pause products for admin review and may require renewed acceptance. Suspension preserves existing order/resolution history. Contact GymDrobe admin for account/business closure and record-retention questions.

## Practical limits and launch decisions

- No live supplier identity, commercial acceptance, actual stock, tax rate, courier promise or banking verification is invented by the fixtures.
- Supplier images use a bounded existing-database allowance; no paid image host is created. Remove unreferenced uploads through the API if necessary. Images referenced by approved history/proof are protected.
- Item credits/refunds are merchandise-based and preserve the purchased shipping charge. Shipping-refund policy for mixed/partially cancelled orders needs the owner's confirmed rules before launch. Unassigned purchased items in mixed orders have separate admin-only controls; supplier snapshots and existing ownership are not invented or reassigned.
- Actual provider test-mode acceptance, approved supplier terms, return/shipping responsibilities and device testing remain launch gates. Physical-device camera/store distribution, automated supplier payouts and optional paid features are outside this core release.
- Native app bundles need a new build/install to expose enabled portal code. A website rebuild does not silently replace an installed Android/iPhone bundle. The shared customer bottom navigation remains Home → Categories → Wishlist → Cart → Account.
