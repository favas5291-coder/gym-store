Your photo. Your choice.

This notice explains how the GymDrobe Virtual Try-On feature handles photos.

Before you generate

Uploaded and camera photos are held in the open fitting room. Image quality and person checks run on your device using a locally served model. Preparing a photo removes embedded metadata, including location metadata. Camera access begins only when you choose “Use camera”; closing the camera or fitting room stops the stream.

When you choose “Try It On”

With your permission, GymDrobe sends a prepared copy of your photo and the chosen product photo to FASHN to generate an appearance preview. These photos are used for this requested try-on. Your cart, address, payment information and body measurements are not included in the request.

Storage and retention

  • GymDrobe does not save your photo or generated preview to your account, browser storage or application database. They pass through the server during generation and retrieval.
  • Closing the fitting room or choosing “Remove photo” clears its local copies. A request already sent may still finish at the image service.
  • Job identifiers and product selections expire from job storage after 10 minutes. Rate-limit counters and a keyed hash of the requesting IP expire within 24 hours. A necessary session cookie expires after 24 hours.
  • FASHN processes inputs sent as base64. Its documented policy removes temporary input copies after processing, with a one-day cleanup backstop. This integration requests base64 outputs, which remain retrievable at the provider for up to 60 minutes after completion.
  • FASHN retains request records such as parameters, timestamps and status; base64 image inputs are represented by placeholders. These records are not automatically deleted. Its policy states customer content is not used for model training unless separately opted in.
  • Downloads and shared copies remain on the devices or services you choose. Removing a photo here does not remove those copies.

Read FASHN’s data-retention policy.

Your choices

You can shop without using Virtual Try-On, remove your photo at any time, and decide whether to download or share a result. Only use photos you have permission to process. For questions about this feature, contact GymDrobe through Help.

AI previews illustrate appearance. They can alter details, body proportions, pose or drape. They do not measure physical fit or guarantee how a product will look in real life.
