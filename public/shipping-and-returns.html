Payment and COD advance

When available, checkout offers full online payment through Razorpay or COD with a 10% online advance.

The COD advance is calculated on the product amount after coupon discounts. The remaining balance, including delivery charges, is payable on delivery.

Review the amount payable now and the balance due before paying. Your order page shows the recorded payment status.

Cancellation

You can request cancellation while an order is confirmed or processing. Open My orders and select your order to see available actions.

Cancellation availability is checked before your request is accepted. Once shipping has started, the customer cancellation action is unavailable.

Cancellation and refund completion are separate. For an eligible cancelled order with a recorded online payment, check the refund status in your order details.

Return or exchange requests

Eligible clothing and footwear can have a 7-day return or exchange request window from delivery when confirmed for that product. Check the product page for the supported action, deadline and conditions before buying. Return and exchange eligibility varies by product.

  1. Open My orders or the secure order link provided after guest checkout.
  2. Select the delivered order.
  3. Choose Return / exchange items, if available.
  4. Select items, quantities and the reason.
  5. Submit the request and check the store response.

Supplier-specific exceptions apply. A product may support exchanges only within 3 days (72 hours), returns only, or neither routine option. Supplements and hygiene products have their own recorded eligibility; do not assume a clothing policy applies. Contact support about damaged, incorrect or faulty items, including when a routine action is unavailable.

The policy saved with your purchase governs its routine request options. Later catalogue changes do not replace an existing order’s conditions.

An exchange requires a different available size or colour of the same product. Replacement stock is checked during processing. Submitting a request does not reserve stock.

Requests require review. Submission does not confirm approval, book a collection or issue a refund. Read any product-specific return information before purchase.

If the delivery date is missing or the request action is unavailable, contact support with your order number.

Refund information

Item refund calculations use the selected quantities and discounts allocated to those items, including applicable kit and coupon discounts. Delivery charges are excluded from the item return calculation.

Check your order for the reviewed refund amount and status. A pending request or approved return does not mean money has been refunded.

Refund completion and any transaction reference are recorded separately. Contact support if you need clarification.
